# Team America

A Runtime Republic Study Circle. The name is temporary until the members choose one.

This repository is public. It holds group information only: counts, reviewed study files and meeting notes. No member's personal finances, ever.

## Join Team America

[Join Team America](https://github.com/RuntimeRepublic/Team-America/issues/new?template=join.yml)

You need a free GitHub account and an AI (Claude free works). Takes about 15 minutes.

Steps: [ONBOARDING.md](ONBOARDING.md)

## Post team news

[Post a win, finding or problem](https://github.com/RuntimeRepublic/Team-America/issues/new?template=team-news.yml): Saturday posts, mid-week posts, findings and issues. How it works: [news/README.md](news/README.md)

## Write a letter

Open one card, for one issue. Your own AI writes a fresh letter; do not copy another member's.
Print it, sign it, and post the letter with a photo of the letter and a photo of the envelope.
Steps: [letters/README.md](letters/README.md)

## How to join for Elected Office Holders

1. See the letters to your office ([letters/offices/](letters/offices/)).
2. Claim your office page: email from an official .gov address, or a link from your official website.
3. Reply in the open on the card.
4. Join the national team; your Congressional team is the members in your district plus your office.
5. Check your own ethics rules first.
