# Rules for the letter

These rules are for the member's own AI.

- One issue per letter.
- Write a fresh letter, never another member's wording. Use the member's own details.
- Office addresses and names come only from official sites (senate.gov, house.gov, agency .gov pages). Say the date you checked.
- FOIA: use the agency's FOIA portal or FOIA.gov instructions, cite 5 U.S.C. 552, and record the tracking number in `meta.yml` (`tracking_no`).
- Add the footer below to every letter. Replace `LTR-####`, `<ST>`, `<office-slug>` and `<n>` for this letter. Short runtimerepublic.com links replace these once the website work order is done.
- For an agency with no state, point "All letters to your office" at `https://github.com/RuntimeRepublic/Team-America/blob/main/letters/offices/agencies/<office-slug>.md` instead of the `<ST>` path.
- Warn the member before they upload photos. Crop or cover your return address and any phone number before uploading. Your name and signature may stay.

## Footer

```
This letter is part of open issue LTR-#### from Team America (RuntimeRepublic).
All letters to your office: https://github.com/RuntimeRepublic/Team-America/blob/main/letters/offices/<ST>/<office-slug>.md
This issue: https://github.com/RuntimeRepublic/Team-America/issues/<n>
Offices — how to reply or join: https://github.com/RuntimeRepublic/Team-America#how-to-join-for-elected-office-holders
```

## Starter prompt

Paste this into your own AI, with the card link:

```
Read these rules:
https://github.com/RuntimeRepublic/Team-America/blob/main/letters/AI-RULES.md

This card:
<paste the card link>

Write one fresh letter on this one issue only, in my words, with my details.
Do not copy another member's letter.
Take office names and addresses only from official sites (senate.gov, house.gov, or the agency's .gov pages) and tell me the date you checked.
If this card is a FOIA request, follow that agency's FOIA portal or FOIA.gov, cite 5 U.S.C. 552, and leave a line for the tracking number.
Put the footer from AI-RULES.md on the letter.
Before I photograph anything, warn me: Crop or cover your return address and any phone number before uploading. Your name and signature may stay.
```
